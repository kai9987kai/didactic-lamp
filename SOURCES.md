# Research sources

The current, implementation-specific bibliography is in
[docs/research.md](docs/research.md). It links primary work on reproducible
agent-based modeling, common random numbers, effective biodiversity, and
multidimensional ecosystem resilience, and states how each source informed v5.

The [model specification](docs/model.md) distinguishes implemented mechanics
from unvalidated assumptions and future work. The previous general-purpose
bibliography mixed unrelated thermodynamics, quantum computing, cryptography,
and GPU topics; those are not evidence for this simulator's capabilities.
